Quick verdict

If a closet smells musty, do not start with perfume. Start with a humidity reading. If the closet is just stale and closed-off, a moisture absorber, charcoal bags, cedar, and occasional airflow may be enough. If the closet is wet, stained, actively moldy, or the smell comes back fast, treat it as a maintenance issue and report it.

What to skip until you know the cause

  • Plug-in fragrances: they can mask musty air without reducing humidity.
  • Overstuffing storage bins: tightly packed fabric holds stale air and slows drying.
  • Leaving damp laundry inside: even slightly damp clothes can make a small closet smell bad.
  • Peel-and-stick liners on suspicious walls: they can trap moisture and create move-out trouble.
  • Ignoring repeat moisture: if the reading stays high or the smell returns quickly, products are not the full fix.

The 20-minute reset

  1. Empty the closet floor and pull clothes away from the back wall.
  2. Check for wet spots, stains, peeling paint, and damp baseboards.
  3. Put the hygrometer inside for 24 hours with the door closed.
  4. Wash or air out anything that already smells musty.
  5. Add moisture control first, then charcoal or cedar for odor.
  6. Leave a small air gap between stored items and the wall.

FAQ

How do I fix a musty closet smell in an apartment?

Measure humidity first, remove damp clothing, create air space, then add a moisture absorber and odor absorber. If the smell returns quickly, check for a moisture source and contact maintenance.

Are charcoal bags enough for a damp closet?

Charcoal bags are better for odor than moisture. For a damp closet, use a hygrometer and a moisture absorber first.

Should I use DampRid in a rental closet?

It can be useful in small enclosed closets. Hang or place it where it cannot spill on clothes or flooring, and follow the product instructions.

When is a musty closet a landlord problem?

If you see wet walls, leaks, recurring mold, soft drywall, or high humidity that does not improve, document it and report it rather than just masking the smell.
